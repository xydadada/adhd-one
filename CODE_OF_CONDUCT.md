# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

ADHD One welcomes constructive participation regardless of experience or background. Be respectful, discuss ideas rather than people, accept correction, and protect other people's privacy.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate disruption, doxxing, publishing credentials or private workspace data, and impersonating DeepSeek or another contributor are not acceptable.

## Scope

This standard applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, release comments, and project-related public communication.

## Reporting and enforcement

For an ordinary moderation problem, contact the maintainers through a repository issue or discussion. If a report contains private or safety-sensitive information, use [private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/xydadada/adhd-one/security/advisories/new) and prefix the title with `[Conduct]`.

Maintainers may edit or remove content and may warn, temporarily restrict, or ban participants when necessary. Reports are reviewed on a best-effort basis; retaliation against a reporter is prohibited.

This policy is intentionally concise and may be refined as the contributor community grows.
